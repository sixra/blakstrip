# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

blakstrip is a small project that people trust with sensitive documents. We want contributing to it
to be straightforward and free of hostility, regardless of your experience, background, identity, or
how you found your way here.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Critique the change, not its author.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are missing context, not bad intent.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and say why when you decline one.
- Respect the security policy: report vulnerabilities privately, never in a public issue.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory or sexualised language.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.
- Posting real personal data in issues or test fixtures. Use synthetic data; a redaction tool is
  precisely the wrong place to leak somebody's actual details.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, commits) and anywhere someone is
representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainer through GitHub
([@sixra](https://github.com/sixra)). Reports are handled confidentially, and the person reporting
will not be named without their agreement.

Maintainers may warn, remove content, or block an account. If you believe a decision was wrong, say
so in your reply; it will be reconsidered.

This document is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), simplified for a project of this size.
